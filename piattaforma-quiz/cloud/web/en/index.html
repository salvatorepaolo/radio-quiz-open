Quiz Radiologia: free radiology cases and a DICOM viewer in your browser

An open-source teaching platform for radiology residents and medical students: read real, de-identified studies in a full DICOM viewer, answer, then read the discussion. No installation, no fees, available in English.

Try the casesOpen the free DICOM viewer

What you can do

  • 170+ cases in CT, MRI, X-ray, ultrasound, mammography and digital pathology, from public archives such as The Cancer Imaging Archive.
  • Answer with a confidence level (sure, probable, hypothesis) or write a free-text report, then compare with the expected findings and the discussion.
  • A real DICOM viewer: scrolling, window/level presets, distance, angle and HU measurements, ROI, axial/coronal/sagittal MPR, 3D volume rendering, side-by-side series and prior comparison.
  • Whole-slide images (SVS, NDPI, MRXS, VSI, SCN…) for radiology–pathology correlation.
  • Learning paths — breast imaging and BI-RADS, FIGO staging of cervical cancer on MRI, pulmonary embolism and emergencies, oncologic staging, chest X-ray, cardiac MRI, musculoskeletal and more — with an entry test and a certificate you can add to LinkedIn.
  • Challenges with colleagues on the same cases.
  • In-browser AI: lung nodule detection (MONAI model) and a draft report, both running locally on your computer.

The free DICOM viewer

Open your own studies, a CD or a folder of DICOM files directly in the browser. The images are processed only on your computer — nothing is uploaded — and the viewer also works offline once loaded. It reads whole-slide pathology images too. User manual · Where to find open whole-slide images.
