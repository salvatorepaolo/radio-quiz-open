Open digital slides: where to download them

Public archives with digital pathology slides to download and open in the viewer: everything happens in the browser, files are not sent to anyone. Licences and terms are those stated by the archives: always check them at the source before using the data.

Before using them

For personal study it is enough to respect the licence. To put a slide in a case of the platform you need a licence that allows redistribution (CC0, CC BY, MIT) and the citation of the source; label and overview must be removed with strumenti/prepara_vetrino.py (or anonimizza_vetrino.py for DICOM). Data under non-commercial (NC) or no-derivatives (ND) licences stay for personal study.
